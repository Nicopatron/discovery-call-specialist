# Examples

Three discovery calls, three syntheses. The transcripts below are **synthetic** — composites built from common discovery patterns, not recordings of any real call. Use them to calibrate the specialist's behavior or to test the folder cold (paste a transcript, ask "Synthesize this discovery call," compare the output to what's documented here).

The outputs follow `rules.md` exactly: archetype first, red flags second when present, three pains with quotes and JTBD, pricing anchor with signal reasoning, ≤150-word follow-up email in the buyer's language, three specific call dates, three prioritized next moves.

## Example 1 — US SaaS founder (English)

### Transcript

```
[Marcus Chen — founder of Pulsemetrics, marketing analytics SaaS, 12 employees, NYC]

Hey, thanks for jumping on. Quick context. We're Pulsemetrics, marketing
analytics for ecommerce. Twelve people, mostly engineers, two on growth.
We've been growing fine but — and I'll be direct — three competitors
launched AI features in Q1 and I'm losing demos to "but does it have AI?"
questions. I need to ship something. ASAP.

I'm not even sure what AI features I want. My head of growth thinks chatbot
for onboarding, my CTO thinks predictive churn, my cofounder thinks an
"AI insights" tab. Honestly I think we just need *something* visible. We
have customer data, plenty of it.

Budget — I'd be willing to invest five to ten K on a first phase. I want
a partner who can move fast and not waste time. We've talked to two
consultancies and they wanted to do six weeks of discovery before writing
code. We don't have six weeks. I have a board meeting in eight weeks and
I want to demo something there.

Decision-making — it's me, but I'd loop in my CTO on technical scope.
He's pragmatic, he'll ask hard questions but he won't block. We've had bad
experiences with agencies before — one took our money for a "growth audit"
that was a 40-page deck of stuff we already knew. I'm allergic to that.

What I want from you on this call: tell me if you can help. If yes, what
would the first phase look like. If no, who would you recommend.

Timezone — we're in NYC. Available basically any morning before 11.
Cool? Let's go.
```

### Output

## Prospect Archetype

**Skeptical-but-frustrated SaaS founder.** Signals: competitive panic ("losing demos to AI questions"), prior bad agency experience ("40-page deck"), urgency ("eight weeks to board demo"), no clear AI use case yet but genuine willingness to build, decision-maker on the call with CTO accessible.

## Red Flags

- **No clear AI use case yet.** *"I'm not even sure what AI features I want."* Not a deal-breaker — Marcus admits it openly — but it means the first deliverable cannot be "the AI feature." It has to be the *decision* about which AI feature, validated against his customer data and his prospects' jobs-to-be-done. If we skip this and build a chatbot because his head of growth said so, we deliver something that doesn't move the board demo needle. Pricing should reflect a discovery-then-build sequence, not pure build.

## Top 3 Pains

**Pain 1 — Surface:** *"three competitors launched AI features in Q1 and I'm losing demos."*
- Real pain: status risk — being seen as the laggard SaaS in a category that's moving on AI.
- JTBD: "give me a credible, demoable AI story for my board and my prospects within eight weeks."

**Pain 2 — Surface:** *"my head of growth thinks chatbot, my CTO thinks predictive churn, my cofounder thinks an AI insights tab."*
- Real pain: internal alignment — three smart people with three different intuitions and no framework to choose between them. Marcus is making the call alone and isn't sure.
- JTBD: "help me pick the right AI feature based on data, not opinion."

**Pain 3 — Surface:** *"two consultancies wanted to do six weeks of discovery before writing code."*
- Real pain: trust deficit toward consultants generally, fear of paying for slides.
- JTBD: "show me code, not decks. Move fast, charge fairly, no bloat."

## Pricing Anchor

**$4K–$8K USD for Phase 1.** Signals:
- Company size (12 people, mid SaaS) → $3–7K base range.
- Urgency (8-week hard deadline, "ASAP") → +30% multiplier → upper end of base.
- Decision-maker on call, CTO accessible → no penalty.
- Prior bad-agency experience → +10% trust premium (he'll need ongoing visible proof of work, not deck-based reassurance).
- No referral, cold demo flow → standard close cycle.

Recommended scope for Phase 1: 2-week feature-prioritization sprint (data audit + 5 customer interviews + scored ranking of three candidate AI features) → exit with a written go/no-go for the board demo. Build phase priced separately based on the chosen feature.

## Follow-up Email

> Subject: Pulsemetrics × AI — next step
>
> Marcus,
>
> Quick recap: you have eight weeks, three internal opinions, and a board demo to clear. You've been burned by agencies before and you want to see code, not decks. Got it.
>
> Here's how I'd open Phase 1 — a two-week sprint that ends with a written, ranked recommendation: which of the three candidate AI features (chatbot / churn / insights) actually moves the needle for Pulsemetrics, based on your data and your customers' workflows. $4K–$6K depending on data access. By week three you'd have a clear yes/no for the board, and we'd be in a position to scope the build with confidence.
>
> Three call windows below, NYC time. Pick whichever works.
>
> — Nico

## 3 Proposed Call Dates

- **Wednesday, May 7 — 9:00 AM EST** (NYC)
- **Thursday, May 8 — 10:30 AM EST**
- **Monday, May 12 — 8:30 AM EST**

## Next Moves

1. **Send the follow-up email within 4 hours** of the call, while urgency is hot. Email *is* the proposal — no separate deck.
2. **Do NOT send a long proposal.** Marcus is allergic. If he wants more detail, he'll ask.
3. **If he confirms a call window, prepare a 1-page Phase 1 contract** — scope, deliverables, price, timeline, off-ramp clause. Sign within 5 business days or move on.

## Example 2 — Clínica dental Buenos Aires (Español)

### Transcript

```
[Mariana Acosta — dueña de Clínica Acosta Odontología, Recoleta CABA,
4 odontólogos, ~200 turnos/mes]

Hola, qué tal. Mirá, te cuento. Tengo la clínica desde el 2014, somos
cuatro odontólogos, atendemos en Recoleta. El problema básico es que
estamos perdiendo turnos por mal manejo de la agenda — la chica que
coordina hace lo que puede pero usa Excel y WhatsApp y se le pasan cosas.
Llegan pacientes que no están en agenda, otros que estaban anotados
nadie les confirmó y no vienen. La semana pasada perdimos como diez
turnos, no es plata bárbara pero es ridículo.

Mi marido me dice que tendría que poner "algo con IA". Yo no entiendo
mucho de eso pero veo que todo el mundo habla. Mi cuñada me pasó tu
contacto, dijo que vos hiciste algo en su clínica de estética el año
pasado y que los ayudaste con la captación. Por eso te estoy hablando.

Lo que quiero es: que la agenda funcione, que los pacientes confirmen
automático, que si alguien no viene se le mande un recordatorio. Si se
puede que respondan WhatsApp solos para preguntas básicas tipo "qué
precio tiene una limpieza" estaría buenísimo.

Plata — no tengo un número fijo. Mi cuñada me dijo que con vos había
gastado tipo dos lucas verdes. Yo puedo manejar eso si veo que sirve.
No tengo apuro, ahora estamos en mayo, si para julio funciona estaría
perfecto.

¿Quién decide? Yo. Mi marido opina pero no se mete. Tendría que
mostrarle algo cuando esté listo.

¿Cómo seguimos? Decime.
```

### Output

## Arquetipo del Prospecto

**Operadora abrumada de servicio profesional.** Señales: clínica en marcha hace 10 años con problema operativo crónico (no estratégico), dolor concreto cuantificado ("perdimos como diez turnos la semana pasada"), referida por un caso exitoso (cuñada → clínica de estética), tomadora de decisión ella misma, sin urgencia ("si para julio funciona perfecto"), no técnica pero práctica.

## Top 3 Dolores

**Dolor 1 — Superficie:** *"Estamos perdiendo turnos por mal manejo de la agenda... la semana pasada perdimos como diez turnos."*
- Dolor real: la operación diaria depende de una persona y un Excel. Mariana no se siente con control de su propio negocio.
- JTBD: "que la agenda funcione sin que yo tenga que estar arriba todo el tiempo."

**Dolor 2 — Superficie:** *"Mi marido me dice que tendría que poner 'algo con IA'. Yo no entiendo mucho de eso pero veo que todo el mundo habla."*
- Dolor real: presión social-familiar de "ponerse al día" + miedo a quedar atrás. No es deseo de IA, es deseo de no quedar como que no entiende.
- JTBD: "darle a mi marido y a mí una respuesta cuando alguien me pregunte qué hago con la tecnología en la clínica."

**Dolor 3 — Superficie:** *"Si alguien no viene se le mande un recordatorio... que respondan WhatsApp solos para preguntas básicas."*
- Dolor real: la coordinadora ya está saturada. Mariana no quiere contratar otra persona, quiere que la actual deje de quemarse.
- JTBD: "automatizar las preguntas repetidas y los avisos sin sumar humanos."

## Anclaje de Precio

**$2K–$4K USD para Fase 1.** Señales:
- Tamaño (4 odontólogos, ~200 turnos/mes, single-location) → rango base $1–3K (PYME chica).
- Sin urgencia (deadline julio = 8 semanas) → sin multiplicador de urgencia.
- Referencia directa de cliente exitoso (cuñada) → close más rápido y trust premium ya pagado por la referida.
- Decisión clara, ella sola → sin penalización.
- Hint de presupuesto ("dos lucas verdes" = USD ~$2K) → ancla $2K es realista para scope mínimo, $4K si se suma WhatsApp bot a la confirmación de turnos.

Scope sugerido Fase 1: integración del sistema de agenda con WhatsApp + recordatorios automáticos 48h y 24h + confirmación con un click + bot básico de FAQ ("limpieza, blanqueamiento, urgencias, horarios"). Quedan afuera: historia clínica digital, integración con obra social, reportería avanzada.

## Email de Follow-up

> Asunto: Clínica Acosta — siguientes pasos
>
> Mariana,
>
> Gracias por la charla. Te recapitulo lo que escuché: estás perdiendo turnos por una agenda que depende demasiado de Excel + WhatsApp y de una sola persona, y querés que para julio eso esté resuelto sin sumar gente al equipo.
>
> Lo que te propongo de Fase 1 es bien concreto: agenda conectada a WhatsApp con recordatorios automáticos a 48h y 24h, confirmación de turno con un click, y un bot de preguntas frecuentes (precios, urgencias, horarios). Inversión: USD 2.500–3.500 dependiendo del sistema actual de agenda. Tres a cuatro semanas de implementación.
>
> Te paso tres horarios para una segunda llamada y avanzar. Cuando confirmes mando una propuesta de una página.
>
> Saludos,
> Nico

## 3 Fechas Propuestas

- **Lunes 12 de mayo — 11:00 AM** (Buenos Aires)
- **Miércoles 14 de mayo — 16:00 PM**
- **Viernes 16 de mayo — 10:30 AM**

## Próximos Pasos

1. **Mandar el follow-up dentro de 24h.** El trust de la referida es perecedero — si tarda más de 48h, la cuñada queda mal y Mariana también.
2. **Antes de la próxima llamada, averiguar qué sistema de agenda usa.** Si es Doctoralia o similar, hay API y la integración es directa. Si es Excel puro, agregar 1 semana de scope para migrar a un sistema mínimo (Cal.com / Doctoralia free tier).
3. **Si confirma, mandar propuesta de UNA PÁGINA en 48h.** Mariana es dueña-operadora; no le interesa leer 12 páginas. Una página: scope, deliverables, plata, cronograma, condiciones de salida.

## Example 3 — Bodega Mendoza scaling DTC US (Spanish-English mixed)

### Transcript

```
[Federico Corti — dueño de Bodega Vista Andes, Mendoza,
boutique malbec exporting to US]

Bueno Nico, gracias. Te cuento rápido. Soy Federico, mi familia tiene la
bodega desde el 2008, hacemos malbec premium y blends. We export to about
18 states in the US, mostly direct-to-consumer through our online shop
and three importers. The thing is — el negocio en US está creciendo bien
pero el customer journey is a mess. People buy once, never hear from us
again, repurchase rate is like 12% which is terrible for wine.

Lo que me interesa: I want to set up some kind of automated, personalized
email and WhatsApp follow-up. La idea es que después de que alguien
compra, hay una secuencia que les manda info del vino, recetas para
maridar, invitaciones a tastings virtuales, etcétera. Right now we send
a "thank you" email and that's it. Nothing else. Es una vergüenza la
verdad.

Sobre IA — vi en LinkedIn que estás haciendo cosas con AI consulting.
No sé si es para mí, pero si AI puede ayudar a personalizar mejor (tipo,
"este cliente compró malbec, mandémosle contenido de malbec, no de
blanco"), bienvenido. No quiero un chatbot por chatbot.

Budget — between $5K and $10K USD for the setup, plus a small monthly
retainer maybe. We invoice everything in dollars, no problem there. The
team is me + my wife handling marketing + an external developer in
Argentina who maintains the website.

Decision is mine but my wife handles the actual marketing copy so she'll
need to be onboard. Timezone — Mendoza, but I'm fluent in English and
most of my US conversations are at 10 AM ET which is 11 AM Mendoza in
May. ¿Qué te parece, vamos para adelante?
```

### Output

## Prospect Archetype

**Cross-border SMB scaler.** Signals: family-owned LATAM business (winery since 2008) selling to US market direct-to-consumer, clear concrete metric ("12% repurchase rate"), already exporting (proven cross-border ops), bilingual fluent owner with US-aligned working hours, decision-maker on call with named secondary stakeholder (wife on marketing), realistic budget signal in USD, no AI hype-seeking — wants outcome.

## Top 3 Pains

**Pain 1 — Surface:** *"People buy once, never hear from us again, repurchase rate is like 12% which is terrible for wine."*
- Real pain: leaving real money on the table on the lifecycle side. Wine is an emotional, repeat-purchase category; 12% means the brand isn't capturing the post-purchase relationship.
- JTBD: "turn first-time US buyers into a repeat customer base I can predict."

**Pain 2 — Surface:** *"Right now we send a 'thank you' email and that's it. Nothing else. Es una vergüenza la verdad."*
- Real pain: mismatch between premium positioning ("malbec premium and blends") and the digital experience post-sale. Federico is embarrassed — that energy is fuel.
- JTBD: "make the digital follow-up match the bottle in the customer's hand."

**Pain 3 — Surface:** *"Si AI puede ayudar a personalizar mejor... bienvenido. No quiero un chatbot por chatbot."*
- Real pain: he's seen the AI hype and is allergic to gimmicks. He wants segmentation and personalization that respects the brand, not a robot pretending to talk to customers.
- JTBD: "use AI where it actually personalizes; nowhere else."

## Pricing Anchor

**$5K–$10K USD for Phase 1, plus optional monthly retainer.** Signals:
- Mid-stage SMB, multi-channel (DTC + 3 importers, 18 US states) → $3–7K base, multi-channel complexity pushes upper end.
- Premium category (boutique malbec) → trust is everything; deliverable quality must match the bottle.
- Stated budget ($5–10K USD) → he set the anchor; meet him at $6–8K for Phase 1, leave retainer as separate line.
- Decision-maker on call + named secondary (wife) → no penalty.
- Refers to LinkedIn signal (warmer than cold lead) → faster close, no premium added.
- Invoices in USD already → no FX friction, same as US clients.

Phase 1 scope: post-purchase email + WhatsApp sequence (5–7 touches over 90 days), customer segmentation by varietal preference, basic personalization layer (NOT a chatbot), virtual-tasting invitation flow tied to segment. Out of scope: full CRM rebuild, paid acquisition strategy, importer-channel automation.

## Follow-up Email

> Subject: Vista Andes × post-purchase journey — next step
>
> Federico,
>
> Recap: 12% repurchase is the number to move, the post-purchase experience right now is one "thank you" and silence, and you want personalization (varietal-aware, brand-respectful) without chatbot gimmicks. Got it. Tu mujer maneja la copy → la incluimos desde la primera llamada de scope.
>
> Phase 1 I'd open at $6K–$8K USD: a 5–7 touch post-purchase sequence (email + WhatsApp), segmented by varietal, with a virtual-tasting hook in week 6. Six-week build, two-week soft launch with 100 customers, then full rollout. Optional retainer for ongoing list management at $800/month.
>
> Tres horarios abajo, Mendoza time which is your team's slot.
>
> — Nico

## 3 Proposed Call Dates

- **Tuesday, May 13 — 11:00 AM** (Mendoza / 10:00 AM ET)
- **Thursday, May 15 — 2:30 PM** (Mendoza / 1:30 PM ET)
- **Monday, May 19 — 11:00 AM** (Mendoza / 10:00 AM ET)

## Next Moves

1. **Send the follow-up email today**, in the same bilingual register he used. Federico mixed Spanish and English fluently — matching that tone signals fit.
2. **Include his wife on the next call invite.** He named her as the marketing operator. Without her in the room from call two, scope drift on copy is guaranteed.
3. **Pre-call, ask for read-only access to his Shopify (or equivalent) and the current "thank you" email.** Walking into call two with a screenshot of the actual current state changes the conversation from "what should we do" to "here's what's broken, here's the fix."
