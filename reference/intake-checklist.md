# Intake Checklist

Before producing the 7-section synthesis (plus Decision Trace), the specialist confirms 5 inputs are present in the transcript. If 4 of 5 are missing or weak, **refuse to synthesize.** Ask for the gaps in one short message instead of producing a plausible-but-fabricated output.

Reason: a guessed pricing anchor on a real discovery call costs more than a clarification email. Hallucinated detail in the email or the next-moves section damages trust the moment the prospect notices.

---

## The 5 required inputs

| # | Input | Why it's required | Example signal | What "weak/missing" looks like |
|---|-------|-------------------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | **Prospect name + role** | Without it, archetype is anonymous and the follow-up email has no addressee. | *"Marcus Chen, founder of Pulsemetrics"* | *"Tengo una empresa"* / *"I run a company"* |
| 2 | **Industry + geography** | Anchors and red flags vary by industry (clínica vs SaaS vs bodega) and by region (US vs LATAM vs cross-border). | *"marketing analytics SaaS, NYC"* / *"clínica dental, Recoleta CABA"* | *"a couple of countries"* / no industry mentioned |
| 3 | **At least one pain, quantified, with a verbatim quote** | Without a quote, the synthesis is generic. Without quantification, pricing has no basis. | *"perdimos como diez turnos la semana pasada"* / *"repurchase rate is like 12%"* | *"things are kind of messy"* / *"marketing está descontrolada"* |
| 4 | **One budget signal** | Pricing anchor needs a frame — either a number, a range, or an explicit absence ("no idea yet, what's typical?"). | *"between $5K and $10K USD"* / *"dos lucas verdes"* | No mention of money at all |
| 5 | **Decision-maker clarity** | Changes deal motion entirely (single decider → faster close; co-sign → expect delay; committee → expect months). | *"Decision is mine but my wife handles the marketing copy"* | *"Mi socia dice X"* without specifying veto / advisory / co-sign rights |

---

## What the specialist does when inputs are missing

1. **Lists which inputs are missing**, in plain language, citing the criteria above by number.
2. **Asks for them in one short message** — no preamble, no pitch, no implied judgment of the prospect.
3. **Defaults output language to English** if the transcript's dominant language is unclear (mixed EN/ES with no clear dominant).
4. **Refuses to produce sections 1–8 of the output contract** until ≥4 of 5 inputs are present.
5. **Surfaces any related objection-bank.md hits** that the operator should be ready for in the next round (e.g., if the prospect mentions a failed prior tool, flag it as objection #2 territory).

The user (consultor) can override the gate explicitly if they want a "best-effort guess" — but the specialist will mark every guessed line with `⚠ guessed — confirm before sending`.

See `examples.md` Example 4 for the canonical refusal flow.

---

## What this is NOT

- **Not** a checklist for scoping a paid engagement (that's a separate document — see `follow-up-templates.md` scope skeleton).
- **Not** a hard rule for short transcripts that genuinely have all 5 inputs in 100 words. A tight 110-word transcript with all 5 signals is fine; a verbose 600-word transcript with none of them is not.
- **Not** an excuse to ask for a CV. The 5 inputs are the minimum to synthesize honestly. Anything beyond is the operator's call.
